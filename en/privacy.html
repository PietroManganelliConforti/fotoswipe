Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Short version

FotoSwipe does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your photos, videos and decisions stay on your device.

Your photos

FotoSwipe reads your photo library only on your device, to show it as cards and to find screenshots, duplicates, similar or blurry photos. No photo, thumbnail or analysis result ever leaves your device. Deletion always happens through your phone's own confirmation dialog. Video compression, moving photos into albums, the widget and reminders also work only on the device.

Data stored on your device

Your decisions (keep, trash, later), progress, statistics and settings are saved in a private database inside the app. Uninstalling FotoSwipe deletes them.

Network connections

FotoSwipe has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It connects to the network only for: purchases and subscription status, handled by Google Play or the App Store through our payment provider RevenueCat (which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, never your photos); and app updates delivered by Expo (EAS Update), which download new app code without sending your data.

Children

FotoSwipe is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. For purchase records, contact Google Play or Apple.

Contact

Questions? Write to the developer at the address shown on the store listing. You can also open a public issue on GitHub.
