Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Short version

FotoSwipe has no account and never asks for your name, email or anything else that identifies you. Your photos, videos and choices stay on your device. For purchases and app updates, only the few technical details described below leave your phone, never your photos.

Your photos

FotoSwipe reads your photo library only on your device, to show it as cards and to find screenshots, duplicates, similar or blurry photos. Duplicates, similar and blurry photos are found by image analysis on your phone; faces, text and objects are recognized by Google ML Kit's AI models, which also run on your phone. No photo, thumbnail or analysis result ever leaves your device. Deletion always asks for your confirmation first. Photo and video compression, moving photos into albums, the widget and reminders also work only on the device.

Data stored on your device

Your decisions (keep, trash, later), progress, statistics and settings are saved in a private database inside the app. They are not included in your phone's Google backup. Uninstalling FotoSwipe deletes them. The thumbnails FotoSwipe makes to show your photos stay in the app's cache, kept under about 150 MB.

Network connections

FotoSwipe has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It connects to the network only for: purchases and subscription status, handled by Google Play or the App Store through our payment provider RevenueCat (which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, never your photos); app updates delivered by Expo (EAS Update), which receives a random installation ID, the app version and the operating system, never your photos; and a one-time download of Google ML Kit's AI models (faces, text, objects) through Google Play services. Google Play services may send Google anonymous diagnostics and usage statistics about ML Kit; your photos and analysis results are never sent.

Children

FotoSwipe is not directed to children under 13 and asks no one for personal data.

Your rights

We keep no data that identifies you, so there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. For purchase records, contact Google Play or Apple.

Contact

The data controller is the developer of FotoSwipe, as named on the app's Google Play listing. Questions or requests? Write to botslyco@gmail.com. You can also open a public issue on GitHub.
