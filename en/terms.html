The app

FotoSwipe helps you review your photo library and move items you choose to your phone's trash. FotoSwipe never deletes anything without your confirmation.

Your responsibility

You decide what to delete. Deleted items stay in your phone's "Recently deleted" (or trash) for about 30 days, depending on your device. Keep a backup of anything important.

Premium

Swiping, collections and stats are free. The first 20 deletions are free; beyond that, and to compress videos, you need FotoSwipe Premium, available as a weekly or yearly subscription (yearly with a free trial). Prices are shown in your currency before you buy. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, from your store account settings.
