How deletion works

Photos and videos deleted from your phone usually stay in its “Recently deleted” for about 30 days, depending on your device. Android 10 has no “Recently deleted”, so every deletion there is permanent. If you choose “Delete right away” or “Free now”, photos and videos are also erased permanently and cannot be recovered. Compression makes a lighter, lower-quality copy and moves the original to the Trash: if you then delete the original from your phone, only the compressed copy is left.
